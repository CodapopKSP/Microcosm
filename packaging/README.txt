MICROCOSM
An evolution simulator: a living ocean, grown from a single microbe.

HOW TO RUN
  Windows: double-click Microcosm.exe
  macOS:   open Microcosm.app. The first time, macOS says it can't check it
           (the app isn't registered with Apple): click Done, then go to
           System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
           "Open Anyway" next to Microcosm. (On macOS 14 and older you can
           instead right-click the app and choose Open.)
  Linux:   run ./microcosm

Press H in the game for the controls. Documentation, on the main menu, explains
how the world works ("Open as PDF" there opens it in your PDF viewer).

WHERE YOUR FILES GO
Saves, the maps you make in the map editor and the organisms you make in the
genome editor are kept in your user folder, not next to the game, so you can
update or move the game freely:
  Windows: %APPDATA%\Microcosm
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Microcosm
  Linux:   ~/.local/share/microcosm
